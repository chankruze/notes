# Salary Reduction

You are all aware of the current economic situation so I don’t need to elaborate on that. A consulting company like BigBinary depends on new projects and I expect getting new projects is going to be hard for a year or so.

For the next year or so people will generate mountains of code using AI and the vast majority of the code will do its job. Until everything becomes too complicated and then things will not work properly. Using AI some of these problems will be alleviated but I believe for some of the problems companies would need experts to debug them. 

I believe we need to wait out this period and then BigBinary will get more projects. At least that’s my theory. Will see what the reality holds.

Until we get more projects we need to make the current revenue last as long as possible to support both our consulting work and the product work. Towards that I’m making following changes to the salary effective July 1, 2026.

# Salary reduction slabs

For the purpose of this discussion I have divided folks into three bands.

* Band 1: People earning upto and including 19 LPA.
* Band 2: People earning 19+ and upto and including 49 LPA.
* Band 3: People earning 49+ LPA.

# Salary reduction percentage

* Band 1: No change in salary
* Band 2: 16% reduction
* Band 3: 20% reduction

# What's my new salary

For the last 14 years everyone's salary has been an integer but that changes with this. You need to multiply your salary with 0.84 and 0.80 depending on your band and that's your new salary. 

For example if someone was earning 28 LPA then the new salary will be 23.52 LPA.
Salary of 38 LPA becomes 31.92 LPA.
Salary of 48 LPA becomes 40.32 LPA.
Salary of 52 LPA becomes 41.60 LPA.

# When is this effective from
This is effective from Q3. It means it’s effective from July 1, 2026. It means the paycheck received in the month of August will be of lesser amount.

# How long this reduction will last
It’s very hard to say at this time. It all depends on when the economic situation for the consulting company improves.
