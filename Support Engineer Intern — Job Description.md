## About PoojaPatha

PoojaPatha is a digital sanctuary of Vedic wisdom — a platform that connects families with verified pandits for authentic rituals, complete samagri support, and a transparent spiritual service experience. We operate across web and mobile platforms serving customers, partners (pandits), and our internal operations team.
## What You Will Work On

You will work directly under the Tech Lead to help build and maintain PoojaPatha's multi-platform product. Your day-to-day will include:

- Triaging and fixing reported bugs across web and mobile apps.
- Implementing small-to-medium features with guidance.
- Writing and reviewing pull requests.
- Testing features manually before they ship.
- Helping document APIs, flows, and known issues.
- Participating in sprint planning and daily standups.

This is a hands-on engineering role — not QA-only or support-ticket-only.
